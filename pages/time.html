Time Vocabulary
Tag Japanese Romaji English Notes
今日きょう kyō today Used to talk about the current day.
Kyō wa getsuyōbi desu.
Today is Monday.
明日あした ashita tomorrow Used to talk about the next day.
Ashita wa gakkō e ikimasu.
I will go to school tomorrow.
昨日きのう kinō yesterday Used to talk about the previous day.
Kinō wa ame deshita.
Yesterday it rained.
朝あさ asa morning Refers to the morning.
Asa ni pan o tabemasu.
I eat bread in the morning.
昼ひる hiru daytime / noon Refers to the daytime or around noon.
Hiru ni hirugohan o tabemasu.
I eat lunch at noon.
夜よる yoru night Refers to the night.
Yoru ni nemasu.
I go to bed at night.
午前ごぜん gozen AM / before noon Goes before the hour.
Watashi wa gozen shichiji ni okimasu.
I wake up at 7:00 AM.
午後ごご gogo PM / after noon Goes before the hour.
Watashi wa gogo sanji ni benkyō shimasu.
I study at 3:00 PM.
正午しょうご shōgo noon (12:00 PM) Refers to exactly 12:00 PM.
Watashi wa shōgo ni hirugohan o tabemasu.
I eat lunch at noon.
今いま ima now Refers to the present moment.
Ima wa kuji desu.
It is 9:00 now.
毎朝まいあさ maiasa every morning Used for repeated morning actions.
Maiasa kōhī o nomimasu.
I drink coffee every morning.
毎晩まいばん maiban every evening Used for repeated evening actions.
Maiban terebi o mimasu.
I watch TV every evening.
毎日まいにち mainichi every day Used for daily habits.
Mainichi Nihongo o benkyō shimasu.
I study Japanese every day.
毎週まいしゅう maishū every week Used for weekly habits.
Maishū toshokan e ikimasu.
I go to the library every week.
毎月まいつき maitsuki every month Used for monthly events.
Maitsuki hon o kaimasu.
I buy a book every month.
毎年まいとし maitoshi every year Used for yearly events.
Maitoshi Nihon e ikimasu.
I go to Japan every year.
～時～じ ~ji o'clock Added after the hour.
Gakkō wa hachiji ni hajimarimasu.
School starts at 8:00.
～分～ふん・～ぷん ~fun / ~pun minute Added after the number. The reading changes depending on the number.
Basu wa juppun de kimasu.
The bus comes in 10 minutes.
半はん han half Replaces 30 minutes.
Ima wa sanji han desu.
It is 3:30 now.
～前～まえ ~mae before Goes after the amount of time.
Densha wa hachiji gofun mae ni demasu.
The train leaves 5 minutes before 8:00.
～後～あと ~ato after Goes after the amount of time.
Watashi wa ima kara juppun ato ni shuppatsu shimasu.
I will leave 10 minutes from now.
～ごろ  ~goro around (approximately) Goes after the time.
Watashi wa shichiji goro ni kaerimasu.
I go home around 7:00.
～から  ~kara from Marks the starting time.
Gakkō wa hachiji kara desu.
School starts at 8:00.
～まで  ~made until Marks the ending time.
Gakkō wa sanji made desu.
School is until 3:00.
～から～まで  ~kara ~made from … to … Used to express a time range.
Gakkō wa hachiji kara sanji made desu.
School is from 8:00 to 3:00.
～時間～じかん ~jikan hour(s) (duration) Used to express a duration.
Nihongo o nijikan benkyō shimasu.
I study Japanese for two hours.

Practice Q&A
Japanese Romaji English
Q: 今何時ですか。 A: 午後一時四十六分です。 Q: Ima nanji desu ka? A: Gogo ichiji yonjūroppun desu. Q: What time is it? A: It is 1:46 PM.
Q: 何時ですか。 A: 三時です。 Q: Nanji desu ka? A: Sanji desu. Q: What time is it? A: It is 3 o'clock.
Q: 今日、お店は何時から何時までですか。 A: 午前八時から午後二時までです。 Q: Kyō, omise wa nanji kara nanji made desu ka? A: Gozen hachiji kara gogo niji made desu. Q: What time is the shop open today? A: It is open from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
Q: 何時間勉強しましたか。 A: 一時間勉強しました。 Q: Nanjikan benkyō shimashita ka? A: Ichijikan benkyō shimashita. Q: How long did you study? A: I studied for one hour.
Q: 何時間本を読みましたか。 A: 二時間本を読みました。 Q: Nanjikan hon o yomimashita ka? A: Nijikan hon o yomimashita. Q: How long did you read? A: I read for two hours.
Q: 何時間歩きましたか。 A: 一時間半歩きました。 Q: Nanjikan arukimashita ka? A: Ichijikan han arukimashita. Q: How long did you walk? A: I walked for one and a half hours.
Q: お店はいつ開きますか。 A: 月曜日と土曜日は午前八時に開きます。 Q: Omise wa itsu akimasu ka? A: Getsuyōbi to doyōbi wa gozen hachiji ni akimasu. Q: When does the shop open? A: It opens at 8:00 AM on Mondays and Saturdays.
Q: 何時に朝ご飯を食べますか。 A: 午前九時に食べます。 Q: Nanji ni asagohan o tabemasu ka? A: Gozen kuji ni tabemasu. Q: What time do you eat breakfast? A: I eat breakfast at 9:00 AM.
Q: いつシャワーを浴びますか。 A: 朝、よくシャワーを浴びます。 Q: Itsu shawā o abimasu ka? A: Asa, yoku shawā o abimasu. Q: When do you take a shower? A: I often take a shower in the morning.
Q: いつ運動しますか。 A: 月曜日の午後七時に運動します。 Q: Itsu undō shimasu ka? A: Getsuyōbi no gogo shichiji ni undō shimasu. Q: When do you exercise? A: I exercise on Mondays at 7:00 PM.
Q: 何時ごろ晩ご飯を食べますか。 A: 午後十時ごろ食べます。 Q: Nanji goro bangohan o tabemasu ka? A: Gogo jūji goro tabemasu. Q: Around what time do you eat dinner? A: I eat dinner around 10:00 PM.
